### Q1: What is the due date for the Dallas ISD Student and Staff Computing Devices proposal?

### Q2: What are the installation and deployment requirements for the Dallas ISD bid?
**Answer:** Deliveries and deployments must incorporate "white glove services", which includes asset decaling, asset reporting, and etching (specifically required on Laptops). Furthermore, software installation requires Autopilot enrollment for Windows devices and Google Management enrollment for Chromebooks.  
**Citations:**  
- 📄 `Addendum 1 RFP JA-207652 Student and Staff Computing Devices.pdf` (Page 1)
- 📄 `Addendum 1 RFP JA-207652 Student and Staff Computing Devices.pdf` (Page 4)
- 📄 `JA-207652 Student and Staff Computing Devices FINAL.pdf` (Page 3)

### Q3: What is the expected delivery date for Bid JA-207652?
**Answer:** The district anticipates requesting quotes for different projects from awarded vendors starting in September of 2024.  
**Citations:**  
- 📄 `Addendum 1 RFP JA-207652 Student and Staff Computing Devices.pdf` (Page 1)

### Q4: Are there any cooperative purchasing agreements available for the Dallas ISD contract?
**Answer:** Yes, the solicitation includes interlocal agreement and cooperative options for the Central Texas Purchasing Alliance (CTPA) and the Educational Purchasing Cooperative of North Texas (EPCNT), allowing other member districts to utilize the awarded contract.  
**Citations:**  
- 📄 `JA-207652 Student and Staff Computing Devices FINAL.pdf` (Page 19, 20)

### Q5: What are the minimum requirements for the Tier 1 Staff Windows Basic Desktop?
**Answer:** The minimum requirements are:
- Enterprise Management Ready with Autopilot Enrollment
- Processor: Intel i5 14th Gen (or equivalent)
- Display: Minimum 23.8" diagonal Touch Screen
- Memory: 16GB RAM minimum
- Wireless: 802.11AX WiFi-6E and Bluetooth 5.1
- Storage: 512 GB NVMe SSD
- OS: Windows 11 Pro
- Warranty: 3-year minimum  
**Citations:**  
- 📄 `JA-207652 Student and Staff Computing Devices FINAL.pdf` (Page 5)
